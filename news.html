Lazatin Outlines 2024 Vision during Induction Ceremony

June 12, 20245 min read

The Philippine Finance Association formally inducted its 11th President, Carlo Enrico B. Lazatin, at a ceremony held at the Makati Diamond Residences on June 12, 2024. The event was attended by over 300 industry leaders, regulators, and government officials.

In his inaugural address, President Lazatin outlined an ambitious three-pillar agenda for his term: Transparency in regulatory reporting and member disclosures; Digital Transformation of PFA's member services and the launch of the Sovereign Ledger platform; and Sustainable Investment frameworks aligned with global ESG standards.

"We stand at an inflection point in Philippine finance," Lazatin said. "The decisions we make in the next three years will determine whether our industry leads the region's digital economy or merely follows it."

BSP Governor Eli Remolona Jr. delivered the keynote address, commending PFA's role in maintaining industry stability and calling for closer collaboration between the association and the central bank on digital asset regulation.

The ceremony also saw the induction of the new Board of Directors, including Executive Vice President Ma. Victoria P. Cruz and Treasurer Antonio S. Reyes.

Quarterly Economic Outlook: 6.2% GDP Growth Projected for 2024

October 24, 202412 min read

The Philippine Finance Association's Research Division has released its Q4 2024 Economic Outlook, projecting a GDP growth rate of 6.2% for the full year — above the government's 6.0% target and significantly outpacing regional peers.

The report, authored by Director of Research Dr. Elena M. Santos, identifies three primary growth drivers: the rapid adoption of digital payment infrastructure, a recovery in OFW remittances to pre-pandemic levels, and sustained domestic consumption buoyed by easing inflation.

"The Philippine economy has demonstrated remarkable resilience," Dr. Santos noted. "Our member institutions report a 23% year-on-year increase in loan disbursements, particularly in the SME and consumer finance segments."

The report also flags risks including elevated global interest rates, geopolitical tensions in the South China Sea affecting trade routes, and the need for accelerated infrastructure investment outside Metro Manila.

PFA recommends that the BSP maintain its current policy stance while preparing contingency measures for external shocks, and calls on Congress to expedite the passage of the Digital Assets Regulation Act.

New BSP Guidelines on Cross-Border Payment Interoperability

October 18, 20246 min read

The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) has released Circular No. 1189, a draft memorandum detailing the technical and compliance requirements for cross-border QR payment interoperability under the ASEAN Payment Connectivity initiative.

The circular requires all BSP-supervised financial institutions, including PFA member companies, to implement ISO 20022 messaging standards by Q2 2025 and to register with the regional payment hub by Q3 2025.

PFA Director for Legislative Affairs Ramon B. Chua welcomed the guidelines, noting that "interoperability is the foundation of a truly integrated ASEAN financial market." He added that PFA will submit formal comments during the 60-day public consultation period.

Key provisions of the circular include mandatory real-time gross settlement for cross-border transactions above PHP 500,000, enhanced KYC requirements for foreign counterparties, and a new dispute resolution framework for cross-border payment failures.

PFA will host a technical briefing for member companies on November 15, 2024, at the RCBC Plaza in Makati.

AI in Credit Scoring: Mitigating Algorithmic Bias in Lending

October 12, 20248 min read

PFA's Research Division has published a whitepaper examining the ethical implications of artificial intelligence in credit scoring, with a focus on the Philippine market's unique demographic challenges.

The paper, co-authored with researchers from the Ateneo de Manila University, analyzes data from 15 PFA member institutions that have deployed machine learning models for credit assessment. It finds that while AI-driven models improve approval rates for previously unbanked borrowers by up to 34%, they also exhibit measurable bias against certain regional and occupational demographics.

"The promise of AI in financial inclusion is real, but so are the risks," said Dr. Santos. "Without deliberate bias mitigation, we risk automating and amplifying existing inequalities."

The whitepaper recommends a set of best practices including regular bias audits, diverse training datasets, and explainability requirements for adverse action notices — aligning with the SEC's draft guidelines on responsible AI in financial services.

PFA is calling on the BSP and SEC to establish a joint technical working group on AI governance in financial services, with PFA serving as the industry representative.

Sustainable Finance: The Rise of Blue Bonds in Southeast Asia

September 28, 202415 min read

Southeast Asia's blue bond market has grown from virtually nothing in 2020 to over USD 4.2 billion in issuances by mid-2024, with the Philippines emerging as a key player through the Development Bank of the Philippines' landmark USD 500 million blue bond offering.

PFA's Capital Markets Committee has released a comprehensive analysis of the blue bond landscape, examining the performance of existing issuances, the regulatory framework under the SEC's Sustainable Finance Roadmap, and the opportunities for PFA member institutions to participate as issuers and investors.

The report finds that Philippine blue bonds have outperformed conventional bonds by an average of 45 basis points over the past 18 months, driven by strong demand from ESG-focused international investors and a scarcity premium.

"Blue bonds represent a convergence of financial opportunity and national interest," said Director for Digital Innovation Miguel A. Fernandez. "The Philippines, as an archipelago nation, has a natural comparative advantage in maritime-focused sustainable finance."

PFA recommends that member institutions explore blue bond issuances for financing fisheries modernization, coastal infrastructure, and marine conservation projects, and calls on the SEC to streamline the green/blue bond certification process.

PFA Launches Sovereign Ledger Digital Platform for Members

September 15, 20244 min read

The Philippine Finance Association has officially launched the Sovereign Ledger, a proprietary digital platform that provides member companies with real-time access to industry data, regulatory updates, and exclusive research publications.

The platform, developed over 18 months in partnership with a leading Philippine fintech company, features a regulatory calendar with automated alerts, a searchable database of all PFA publications since 1962, a member directory with verified contact information, and a secure document exchange portal for regulatory submissions.

"The Sovereign Ledger is the realization of our digital transformation pillar," said President Lazatin at the launch event. "It transforms PFA from a periodic convener into a daily resource for our members."

The platform is available to all active PFA members at no additional cost. New members who join before December 31, 2024, will receive a complimentary one-year subscription to the PFA Economic Intelligence Report.

Over 200 member companies have already registered on the platform since its soft launch in August, with an average of 450 daily active users.

SEC Issues New Rules on Lending Company Disclosures

August 30, 20247 min read

The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has issued Memorandum Circular No. 12, Series of 2024, mandating enhanced disclosure requirements for all registered lending companies, effective January 1, 2025.

The circular requires lending companies to publish their effective interest rates, all fees and charges, and the total cost of credit in a standardized format on their websites and in all loan documentation. It also mandates quarterly reporting of loan portfolio quality metrics to the SEC.

PFA Director for Legislative Affairs Ramon B. Chua described the circular as "a significant step toward greater transparency in the lending industry," adding that PFA had advocated for standardized disclosure requirements for several years.

"Our members who already practice transparent disclosure will benefit from a level playing field," Chua said. "This circular raises the bar for the entire industry."

PFA will conduct a compliance workshop for member companies on October 10, 2024, covering the specific requirements of the circular and best practices for implementation. Registration is open on the Sovereign Ledger platform.

Philippine Peso Bond Market Reaches PHP 8 Trillion Milestone

August 10, 202410 min read

The Philippine peso-denominated bond market has crossed the PHP 8 trillion milestone for the first time in its history, according to data compiled by PFA's Research Division from Bureau of the Treasury and SEC filings.

Government securities account for PHP 6.1 trillion of the total, while corporate bonds — including those issued by PFA member companies — represent PHP 1.9 trillion, a 28% increase from the same period in 2023.

The growth in corporate bond issuances has been driven by financing companies seeking to diversify their funding sources away from bank borrowings, and by investor appetite for higher-yielding peso-denominated instruments amid a declining interest rate environment.

"The PHP 8 trillion milestone is a testament to the maturity of our capital markets," said Dr. Santos. "But it also highlights the need for continued market development, particularly in the secondary market for corporate bonds."

PFA is working with the Philippine Dealing System and the SEC to develop a more liquid secondary market for corporate bonds, including the introduction of a centralized electronic trading platform for retail investors.
