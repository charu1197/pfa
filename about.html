Current Leadership

Carlo Enrico B. Lazatin

President, Philippine Finance Association · 2022–Present

Carlo Enrico B. Lazatin is a finance leader with extensive experience in lending, financial services, and organizational leadership. As President of the Philippine Finance Association (PFA) and DES Financing Corporation, he plays a key role in advancing industry standards, driving financial inclusion, and strengthening the country's financing ecosystem.

He is recognized for his strategic approach to sustainable growth, governance, and innovation in the financing sector, balancing operational excellence with long-term value creation.

Vision & Advocacy

Committed to building a more inclusive and resilient financial sector in the Philippines, his leadership emphasizes expanding access to fair and transparent financing, strengthening trust between financial institutions and communities, and encouraging innovation while maintaining regulatory discipline.
